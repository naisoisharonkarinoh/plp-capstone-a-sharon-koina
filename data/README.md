# Data provenance, access and ethics

## Source
All three source files (`sales.csv`, `products.csv`, `customers.csv`) were synthetically generated for this educational capstone using Python and NumPy with a fixed random seed. No external dataset or third-party data source was used.

## License and access
The generated data is provided with this repository for educational and portfolio demonstration. It is original synthetic data. Accessed/generated on 9 November 2026. No download URL is required; the generator and CSV files are included.

## Contents
- `raw/sales.csv`: 5,000 simulated transactions.
- `raw/products.csv`: 20 simulated product records and unit prices.
- `raw/customers.csv`: 1,000 artificial customer IDs and broad segment/county labels.
- `processed/`: cleaned sales, dimension tables, and a cleaning log.

## Ethics and privacy
No real customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, exact addresses, payment details, or other direct identifiers are included. Customer IDs are artificial. County and customer segment fields are simulated. The data is not suitable for making claims about any real retailer or population.
